# Responsible Gaming Policy

## Our Commitment

At Beef, we are committed to promoting responsible gaming and providing a safe, entertaining environment for debates and wagering. We recognize that gambling, even with virtual currency, can become problematic for some individuals.

## Gambling Can Be Addictive

### Warning Signs

You may have a gambling problem if you:
- Spend more time or money on betting than you intended
- Feel the need to bet with increasing amounts
- Feel restless or irritable when trying to cut back
- Gamble to escape problems or feelings
- Lie to others about your gambling
- Jeopardize relationships or opportunities due to gambling
- Rely on others to bail you out of financial trouble caused by gambling
- Have made repeated unsuccessful attempts to control gambling

## Get Help

## Self-Help Tools

### 1. Set Limits
Before you start:
- Decide how much virtual currency you're willing to risk
- Set a time limit for your session
- Never chase losses

### 2. Take Breaks
- Step away regularly
- Don't bet when emotional or stressed
- Engage in other activities

### 3. Reality Checks
Ask yourself:
- Am I betting more than I intended?
- Am I neglecting other responsibilities?
- Is gambling affecting my mood or relationships?

### 4. Self-Assessment
Take online quizzes:
- https://www.ncpgambling.org/help-treatment/self-assessment/
- Answer honestly to understand your relationship with gambling

## Tips for Responsible Gaming

### DO:
✅ Set a budget before you start
✅ Treat it as entertainment, not income
✅ Take regular breaks
✅ Gamble only when in a good state of mind
✅ Understand the odds and mechanics
✅ Seek help if you're concerned
✅ Use self-exclusion tools if needed

### DON'T:
❌ Chase losses
❌ Gamble with money you can't afford to lose
❌ Borrow money to gamble
❌ Gamble when under the influence
❌ Use gambling to solve financial problems
❌ Let gambling interfere with work or relationships
❌ Gamble when feeling depressed or anxious

## For Friends and Family

If you're concerned about someone:

### Signs to Watch For:
- Preoccupation with gambling
- Increasing time spent on gambling sites
- Borrowing money or selling possessions
- Mood swings or irritability
- Neglecting responsibilities
- Being secretive about gambling activities

### How to Help:
1. **Talk to them** - Express concern without judgment
2. **Offer support** - Provide resources and be available
3. **Set boundaries** - Don't enable gambling by lending money
4. **Encourage professional help** - Suggest counseling or support groups
5. **Take care of yourself** - Seek support for yourself if needed

### Resources for Families:
- **Gam-Anon** (for families): https://www.gam-anon.org
- **National Council on Problem Gambling**: 1-800-522-4700

## Education and Awareness

Understanding gambling helps prevent problems:

### Key Concepts:
- **House Edge**: The platform's statistical advantage
- **Expected Value**: Long-term average outcome
- **Variance**: Short-term fluctuations
- **Probability**: Actual odds of winning

### The Reality:
- Gambling is entertainment, not investment
- The odds favor the house in the long run
- Winning streaks end
- Losses are part of gambling

## Myths vs. Facts

### MYTH: "I'm due for a win after several losses"
**FACT**: Each bet is independent. Past results don't affect future outcomes.

### MYTH: "I can win back my losses if I keep playing"
**FACT**: Chasing losses usually leads to bigger losses.

### MYTH: "I have a system that works"
**FACT**: No system can overcome mathematical house edge.

### MYTH: "I can stop anytime"
**FACT**: Gambling addiction is real and can happen to anyone.
